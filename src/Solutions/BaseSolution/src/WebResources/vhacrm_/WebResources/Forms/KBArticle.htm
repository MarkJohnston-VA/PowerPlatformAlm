When speaking to a caller who demonstrates any warning signs of suicide: 

·         Contact your Lead for assistance any time you do not feel able to support the caller

·         Stay calm and listen

·         Take threats seriously

·         Allow caller to talk about their feelings

·         Do not place the caller on hold, or transfer the call without permission

 

Call Flow: 

Caller demonstrates at least one of the warning signs of suicide

 

CSR: Ask a question to confirm the intention of the caller

·         “I hear you saying (restate phrase made by caller)”

·         “Are you thinking about hurting yourself?”


Caller responds NO

·         CSR: “I apologize if I have offended you; I just want to confirm that you are doing alright. How can I help you today?


Caller responds YES

Make notes of the following information:

·         Caller name and SSN

·         Ask - What is your location or address right now?

·         Ask - What is the phone number where you are now?


CSR: “I would like to put you in touch with someone who may be able to help you.  Would you wait a moment while I contact the person who can help?”


Warm transfer to Veteran Crisis Line @ 800-273-8255. Stay on the call until the operator takes control of the call.

 

When you reach the Veteran Crisis Line, provide the following information:

·         CSR: “My name is (your name) I am calling from the Veterans Affairs with (caller name).

·         CSR: “(caller name) told me that he/she was thinking about hurting him/her self.

·         CSR: “How can you help us?”

 

If the caller terminates the call and/or you lose the connection, call the Veteran Crisis Line @ 800-273-8255, and provide the following information:

·         Recorded information from step 4

·         Description of your conversation, and why you think the caller may be in danger

Veteran Crisis Line 988 (then press 1)
